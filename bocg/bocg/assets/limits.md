# Limits — BankingOps Coverage Grid (BOCG)

- **Consensus = textual convergence, not economic importance.** A STRONG tier means several frozen models, under
  the same cold prompt, independently named a division that maps to the same key. It says nothing about revenue,
  headcount, or risk weight, and nothing about how "important" the division is.
- **Gaps are not elicited.** Models were never asked where they are weak or strong or what is under-served (I7).
  Competency gaps can only come from measured task scores in a later phase; nothing in this bundle is such a score.
- **Anchors are model-emitted, then corroborated.** An anchor appears in the grid only after a human/agent verified
  it against the actual source; the count of verified anchors is a corroboration statistic, not a market statistic.
- **Seat economics are survey ranges.** `addressable_seat_cost_median` is the median over samples of
  midpoint(cost_per_seat) x determinable_fraction and inherits every limitation of the surveys the models cited.
- **Alias decisions are human.** `aliases.yaml` merges textual variants; a different alias table yields a different
  matrix. The table's sha256 is published so the choice is auditable.
- **Coverage statements cover filled cells only.** `coverage_statement.md` makes no claim about any division that
  is not a filled cell in `grid.json`.
- **Knowledge cut-off.** Models answered "as of" the year given in the prompt; anchors may lag the current state of
  regulation or disclosure.
